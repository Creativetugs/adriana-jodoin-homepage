A thoughtful approach

You don’t have to figure it all out on your own.

Carewithintention

Therapy can be a place to slow down, make sense of what’s happening, and build skills that help life feel more manageable.

My approach is warm, active, and collaborative. Together, we’ll work to understand what’s getting in the way, identify what matters to you, and create practical steps toward meaningful change.

I draw primarily from evidence-based approaches, including cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), while tailoring our work to your needs, goals, and stage of life.

A little about me

Meet Dr. Adriana Jodoin.

I’m a clinical psychologist who believes therapy works best when you feel respected, understood, and actively involved in the process.

I earned my master’s and doctoral degrees in clinical psychology from Yeshiva University and have trained and worked in hospital and academic settings, including NewYork-Presbyterian and Weill Cornell. Alongside my private practice, I teach and supervise psychology and psychiatry trainees.

Outside of work, I enjoy yoga, hiking, cycling, and live music. I returned to Massachusetts in 2024 to be closer to family and enjoy the changing seasons.

A few helpful details.

Starting therapy can bring up questions. Here are a few basics to help you decide on your next step.

Still have a question? Get in touch ↗
Do you offer in-person sessions?

At this time, sessions are virtual. Dr. Jodoin is licensed in New York and Massachusetts and can work with clients located in those states, subject to clinical fit and availability.

Who do you work with?

Dr. Jodoin works with children, adolescents, adults, and parents. The best fit depends on your concerns, goals, and the type of support you are looking for.

What happens when I reach out?

Send a brief inquiry using the contact options below. You can share what you are looking for and ask any initial questions before deciding on next steps.

Do you offer consultation or supervision?

Yes. Dr. Jodoin welcomes inquiries about professional consultation, supervision, and lectures related to her areas of expertise.
